The formulas on this site are written in [UnicodeMath](https://unicodemath.org/) and converted into browser-supported [MathML](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/MathML) by [UnicodeMathML](https://github.com/MurrayIII/UnicodeMathML).

The main purposes of the derivations in this chapter include:
- To explain concepts or formulas.
- To provide real-world content samples for UnicodeMath -> MathML for testing parsing, builds, page layout, and browser rendering.
